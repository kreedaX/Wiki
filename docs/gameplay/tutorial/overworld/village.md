# Overworld: Village
Villages are the seed type that new players will experience the most. They have the highest change of occuring for any rank below emerald, with a 55% chance for coal players, and a 30% chance for iron and gold players.
## Filter Specifications
- Spawns within 7 chunks
  - Always generated at positive coordinates for X and Z
- Contains a guaranteed Blacksmith*
- 3 lava pools are guaranteed near the Village OR the Blacksmith contains 8+ Obsidian for an enter with Blacksmith lava
- Artificial lava pools look similar to vanilla and are the 3 guaranteed ones! There may be more vanilla lava pools but the artificial ones are guaranteed.
- Taiga villages always have 10+ Obsidian enters (lava pools do still exist)
- Iron Golem always drops 4 Iron (never 3 or 5)
- At least 7 Iron (or 4 Iron + 3 Diamonds) (including golem) with food in chests or haybales

*: The guaranteed Blacksmith is usually a weaponsmith, but can instead be a toolsmith in desert and taiga villages.
## How to Play: Plains, Taiga, Acacia
Make your way towards the blacksmith. It is a good practice to memorize how the weaponsmith/toolsmith looks for each village type.
On the way, loot houses with food and try to grab at least two beds (this guarantees blaze bedding later in the run). Gather wood, kill the golem if necessary, perform necessary tasks, and enther the nether.
## Specifics:
### Plains
Go to the inner right corner of the patio from the outside and mine the log at face height. Upon breaking the log, loot the chest inside. Additionally, you can choose to mine both logs before looting the chest.
If you are approaching from the back, break the glass pane, jump, and open the chest. Proceed to break any oak pillar of the blacksmith to obtain wood.
